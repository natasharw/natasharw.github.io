About

Coast to Coast Walk UK is a map, guide and logbook for Wainwright's route across northern England, about 190 miles from St Bees to Robin Hood's Bay.

Scratch off each day's walking across the Lake District, the Yorkshire Dales and the North York Moors, read up on the stops along the way, and keep a diary of the whole crossing.

What it does

  • Scratch-off map with the full route
  • Guide to stops, villages, valleys and viewpoints
  • Daily distances and progress stats
  • A personal walking diary
  • Syncs across your devices
