About

CookedThis is a cooking journal that works like a fitness tracker. Log what you cook in a couple of taps, add a photo, and build a history of every meal you've made.

Streaks, stats and insights show how often you cook, how many new dishes you've tried and which ones you keep coming back to. It won first place in the OneSignal category at RevenueCat Shipaton 2025.

What it does

  • Log meals in seconds, with photos
  • A recipe history built from what you actually cook
  • Streaks, stats and weekly insights
  • Track new dishes and batch cooks
