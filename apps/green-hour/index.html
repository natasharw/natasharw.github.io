About

Green Hour asks how you feel before you head outside, then again when you come back, and keeps track of the difference between the two.

A check-in takes about ten seconds. Pick up to three feelings and rate how you're doing from 0 to 10. Over a few weeks those changes build into your Nature Delta, a running picture of how much time outside tends to shift how you feel.

What it does

  • Quick check-ins before and after you go outside
  • A feelings wheel with a small set of emotions
  • Sessions you start as you head out and finish when you're back
  • Your Nature Delta and history over time
