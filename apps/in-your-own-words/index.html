About

In Your Own Words is a place to practise the conversations that are hard to start, like giving difficult feedback or saying no to your manager.

Pick the scenario closest to yours, read what tends to make it awkward, then say your opening out loud for up to a minute. The app shows your pace, your filler words, your longest clean run and where you hesitated, so you can try again and watch it get easier.

What it does

  • Scenario cards for difficult work conversations
  • Speak your opening for up to a minute
  • Pace, filler words and hesitations after every run
  • Your runs saved under each conversation, so you can see progress
