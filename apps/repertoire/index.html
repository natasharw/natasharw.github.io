About

Repertoire is a simple place to store, organise and search your favourite recipes.

Sort them by dish type or cooking time, keep the ingredients and method together, and find any recipe again with search. Everything is backed up to iCloud, so your recipes are there on any of your devices.

What it does

  • Organise by dish type and cooking time
  • Search across all your recipes
  • Notes and source links for every recipe
  • iCloud backup across your devices
