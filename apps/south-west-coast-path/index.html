About

South West Coast Path+ is a map, guide and diary for England's longest National Trail, the 630 miles from Minehead to Poole Harbour.

Scratch off each stretch as you walk it, look up the towns, beaches and viewpoints along the way, and keep a diary of your days on the path. Your progress syncs across your devices and the maps work offline.

What it does

  • Scratch-off map with the full route
  • Guide to towns, villages, beaches and viewpoints
  • Distance, coverage and route stats
  • A personal walking diary
  • Offline maps and guide
