About

West Highland Way Explorer is a guide and companion for Scotland's most-walked long-distance trail, 96 miles from Milngavie to Fort William.

Stamp off towns, landmarks and checkpoints as you reach them, follow your progress stage by stage, and build a Highland Passport that records the whole walk.

What it does

  • Scratch-off map of the route
  • Highland Passport stamps for each stop
  • Progress across every stage
  • Background on the places you pass through
