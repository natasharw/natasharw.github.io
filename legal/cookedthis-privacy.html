I Cooked This - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

I Cooked This is a cooking log for iPhone. You record the meals you cook, add photos, build a list of your dishes and look back at what you have made. This policy explains what data the app collects, who processes it, and how you can control it.

There are no accounts. Your dishes, meal logs and photos are stored on your device and are not sent to us.

Who is responsible

I Cooked This is made by Natasha Wilson, an independent app developer trading as a sole trader in the United Kingdom. For the purposes of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, Natasha Wilson is the data controller. Contact: flourishtechcoaching@gmail.com

Data kept on your device

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone or iPad: your dishes and variations, meal logs with their dates, times and tags, your notes, and your photos. Photos are saved as files inside the app. They are not uploaded anywhere and are not backed up by the app to any server. If you back up your device, they are included in that backup under your own Apple settings. All of this is removed when you delete the app.

Camera and photo library

The app asks for access to your camera so you can take a photo of a meal, and to your photo library so you can choose existing photos. The photos you pick or take are copied into the app and are never sent to us or to any third party. You can change these permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Usage analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand which features people use. It records events such as which screens and tabs are opened, when a dish or meal log is created, and when the Premium upgrade screen is opened. Firebase links these events to a random app instance ID. It also receives basic device information such as model, iOS version, language and approximate country.

Some events include an ID for the dish you pick or create, the tags on a dish or meal log, the date you are viewing and, if something goes wrong, the text of the error. The names of your dishes, your notes, your photos and your location are never included.

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and error reports, so problems can be found and fixed. These include the device model, iOS version, app version and details of the error.

The app does not show ads and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Purchases

Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly. Purchases are handled by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, which processes:

  • A random app user ID, so the purchase can be restored on your devices
  • Purchase history and subscription status
  • Country of purchase and basic device information

We never see or receive your payment card details. Those go to Apple alone. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple account settings.

Lawful basis

Analytics and crash reports are used to keep the app working and improve it (legitimate interests). Purchase processing is needed to provide the subscription you buy (contract).

Children

I Cooked This is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will act on it.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EEA, you have the right to access, correct, or erase your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to data portability.

You can edit or delete your dishes, meal logs and photos in the app at any time, and deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. Analytics and purchase data are linked only to random IDs, so to ask about data held by Firebase or RevenueCat, contact me at flourishtechcoaching@gmail.com and include your app user ID if you can find it.

You also have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority. In the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office.

Data retention

Analytics and crash data are kept by Google, and purchase records by RevenueCat and Apple, according to their own published policies, linked above. Nothing else is held by us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
