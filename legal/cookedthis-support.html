Common questions

I subscribed to Premium but it isn't showing

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, making sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with. If it still doesn't appear, get in touch using the address above.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
