Feeling Wheel - Support

Name what you are feeling, using the feeling wheel.

Get in touch

Questions, bug reports, or something not working as it should? Email feelingwheelapp@gmail.com. You can also use Contact Support in the app's Settings tab.

It helps to include your iOS version, your device model, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

How do I log how I'm feeling?

On the Wheel tab, start with the broad feeling in the middle and work outwards to the word that fits best. You can pick up to five feelings for one entry and add a note if you want to.

Where is my data stored? Can I get it back if I lose my phone?

Everything stays on your device. There is no account and no server, so nothing is uploaded, but it also means there is no copy I can restore for you. Your entries are included in an encrypted iPhone or iCloud device backup, so restoring a backup restores Feeling Wheel along with it.

You can also keep your own copy: in Settings, choose Export My Data to save all your entries as a CSV file.

How do I delete an entry?

Open the entry from the History tab, tap the … menu in the top corner, then choose Delete Entry. Deleting the app removes everything at once, since nothing is stored anywhere else.

How do I change the app's language?

Feeling Wheel is available in English, French and Spanish. In Settings, tap Language. This opens the app's page in the iOS Settings app, where you can choose a preferred language. Your history is kept when you switch.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing

In Settings, tap Restore Purchases, making sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. This works for both the subscription and the one-time Lifetime purchase. If it still doesn't appear, email me.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. In Settings, tap Manage Subscription, or open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals and Pro stays active until the end of the paid period. Lifetime is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to renew or cancel.
