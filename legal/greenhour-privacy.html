Green Hour — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Green Hour measures how time outside changes how you feel. This policy explains what data the app handles, who processes it, and how you can control it.

Green Hour has no account system and no server of its own. Your check-ins, ratings, notes, sessions and streaks are stored only on your device. We cannot see them, we never receive them, and there is nothing for you to log in to. The data that reaches a third party comes from purchases, and from anonymous product-usage analytics — never from your check-ins, ratings, notes or emotions.

Who is responsible

Green Hour is made by Natasha Wilson, an independent app developer trading as a sole trader in the United Kingdom. For the purposes of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, Natasha Wilson is the data controller.

Contact: flourishtechcoaching@gmail.com

Data stored on your device only

The following is kept in local app storage, is never transmitted to us, and is deleted when you delete the app:

  • Your check-ins: the emotions you tap and your own 0 to 10 rating
  • Any notes you write on a check-in
  • Your sessions, including start and end times and duration
  • Your daily goal, streak and calculated deltas
  • Your settings: reminder time, appearance preference, and whether you have unlocked Plus
  • A cached copy of your subscription status, so the app works offline

You can delete any individual session, along with its check-ins, from the History tab at any time. Deleting the app removes everything at once.

Apple Health

Green Hour can optionally import your outdoor workouts (walks, runs, rides, swims and hikes) so they become sessions without you having to log them by hand. This is read-only and entirely optional:

  • The app never writes anything to Apple Health.
  • Nothing is imported until you tap the import button yourself.
  • Imported workout data stays on your device, exactly like everything else. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never shared or sold.

You can revoke access at any time in the iOS Settings app, under Privacy & Security, then Health, then Green Hour.

Location

Green Hour works fully without your location, and does not ask for it at launch. It is offered as an optional accuracy upgrade: with it, the app can show your real sunrise and sunset times instead of a rough estimate.

  • Location is only requested when you turn on "Exact sunset times" in Settings, or tap the one-time prompt on the Today screen.
  • The app requests reduced (approximate) accuracy, which is city-level, and takes a single reading rather than tracking you.
  • Nothing runs in the background, and there is no GPS trail, map or route recording anywhere in the app.
  • Your coordinate is used on the device to calculate sun times and is never transmitted to us or stored off your device.

You can turn it off at any time from the same Settings toggle, or in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security, then Location Services.

Notifications

The daily reminder is opt-in. If you enable it, iOS asks your permission and the reminder is scheduled entirely on your device. No notification is sent from a server, because there is no server. If you decline, the app simply leaves the reminder off.

Purchases and subscriptions

Green Hour Plus is an optional subscription (monthly or yearly) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are handled by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, which processes:

  • A randomly generated anonymous app user ID
  • Purchase and subscription history, renewals and expiries
  • Country of purchase and basic device information

This is the only data that leaves your device, and only if you make a purchase. It contains nothing about your check-ins, your ratings, your emotions or your notes. We never see or receive your payment card details — those go to Apple alone. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Green Hour also offers a hardship unlock: if the price is a barrier, tapping "Can't afford it?" on the paywall grants Plus on your device. This requires no proof, no email address and no account, and nothing at all is sent anywhere when you use it.

Growth analytics

Green Hour uses Layers, a third-party analytics and attribution SDK, to understand how people find the app and how its optional growth features (like the monthly recap share card) perform. Layers processes:

  • Anonymous product-usage events — for example, that a session was completed, a paywall was shown, or a subscription started. Event data never includes your check-ins, ratings, notes, emotions or Nature Delta.
  • A device identifier and basic device information, used only to group events, not to identify you personally.

This data is not linked to your identity — Green Hour never sends Layers your name, email or any account identifier, because the app has no account system to draw one from — and is not used to track you across other apps or websites. Green Hour does not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because nothing in the app reads or requests it.

No advertising or behavioural profiling

Green Hour shows no ads of any kind. It does not build a behavioural profile of you, does not use advertising identifiers, and your data is never sold or shared with data brokers.

Children

Green Hour is a general-audience wellbeing app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will act on it.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EEA, you have the right to access, correct, or erase your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to data portability. Because Green Hour has no account system and stores everything only on your device, the fastest way to erase everything we could possibly hold is to delete individual sessions in the app, or delete the app entirely.

For data held by our processors, contact us at flourishtechcoaching@gmail.com and we will pass the request on. You also have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.

Data retention

On-device data is kept until you delete it or delete the app. Data held by Apple and RevenueCat is retained according to their own published policies, linked above. Data held by Layers is retained according to Layers' own policies.

A note on what Green Hour is not

Green Hour is a wellbeing tool. It does not diagnose or treat anything, and it is not a medical device. Your check-ins are your own record of how you feel, kept for you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
