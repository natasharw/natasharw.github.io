Outlie — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Outlie is a daily geography puzzle game for iOS. This policy explains what data the app collects, who processes it, and how you can control it.

The game itself collects nothing. All puzzle content is bundled inside the app, and playing works entirely offline. Your puzzle progress, streak and daily token allowance are stored only on your device. Data collection in Outlie comes from two optional places: advertising and purchases.

Who is responsible

Outlie is made by Natasha Wilson, an independent app developer trading as a sole trader in the United Kingdom. For the purposes of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, Natasha Wilson is the data controller.

Contact: flourishtechcoaching@gmail.com

Data stored on your device only

The following is kept in local app storage, is never transmitted to us, and is deleted when you delete the app:

  • Which puzzles you have solved, and on which days
  • Your current daily streak
  • Your Compass Token balance and daily allowance
  • A cached copy of your subscription status, so the app works offline

Outlie has no account system. We cannot see your progress and there is nothing for you to log in to.

Advertising

Outlie shows rewarded video ads only — ads you choose to watch in exchange for something, such as extra Compass Tokens or temporary access to the puzzle archive. There are no banner or interstitial ads, and no ad is ever shown without you asking for it.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When you watch one, Google may collect and process:

  • Device advertising identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system and app version
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, completions, clicks)

Google shares this with advertising partners in order to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses AdMob data.

Your consent, and how to change it

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area, or a US state with applicable privacy laws, Outlie asks for your consent before requesting any ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. No ads are requested until you have made a choice.

You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from Ad privacy options, in the app's Settings screen (the gear icon on the home screen). Withdrawing consent stops personalised advertising; you may still see non-personalised ads if you continue to choose to watch rewarded videos.

Purchases and subscriptions

Outlie offers an optional subscription (Outlie Pro) and optional token packs. Purchases are handled by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, which processes:

  • A randomly generated anonymous app user ID
  • Purchase and subscription history, renewals and expiries
  • Country of purchase and basic device information

We never see or receive your payment card details — those go to Apple alone. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Outlie is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will act on it.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EEA, you have the right to access, correct, or erase your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to data portability. Because Outlie has no account system and stores progress only on your device, the fastest way to erase everything we could possibly hold is to delete the app.

For data held by our processors, contact us at flourishtechcoaching@gmail.com and we will pass the request on. You also have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.

Data retention

On-device data is kept until you delete the app. Data held by Google and RevenueCat is retained according to their own published policies, linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
