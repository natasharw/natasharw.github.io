Common questions

Does Rehearsal tell me what to say?

No, and it never will. Rehearsal measures how you said something — your pace, filler words, longest fluent stretch, and pauses — not what you said. There's no advice, no model answer, and no AI reading your words.

Is my recording sent anywhere?

No. Speech recognition happens entirely on your device using iOS's on-device speech engine. There is no server in that path — nothing about what you say ever leaves your phone.

Where is my data stored? Can I get it back if I lose my phone?

Everything — recordings, transcripts, results, history — stays on your device. There is no account and no server, so nothing is uploaded, but there is also no backup we can restore from. Your data is included in an encrypted iPhone or iCloud device backup, so restoring a backup restores Rehearsal along with it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete an individual attempt from its results screen or your history. Settings → Delete everything removes every recording, transcript and result at once. Deleting the app does the same.

Why is my free recording limited?

The free tier gives you one recording a day, 7 days of history, and a handful of scenarios across the decks, so you can try the whole loop before buying anything. Rehearsal Pro removes the daily limit and the history cutoff, and unlocks every deck.

I bought a deck or Pro but it isn't showing

Open the paywall and choose Restore purchases (also available in Settings), making sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. If it still doesn't appear, email us.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals and Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.
