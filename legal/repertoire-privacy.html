Repertoire - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Repertoire keeps your recipes in one place. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and I can't see your recipes.

Who is responsible

Repertoire is made by Natasha Wilson, an independent app developer trading as a sole trader in the United Kingdom. For the purposes of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, Natasha Wilson is the data controller.

Contact: flourishtechcoaching@gmail.com

Your recipes

Everything you add to Repertoire (recipe names, meal types, cooking times, website links, cookbook names and notes) is stored on your device.

If iCloud is turned on for Repertoire, your recipes also sync through your own private iCloud account, so they appear on your other iPhones and iPads. Apple stores this data under its own privacy policy. It sits in your private iCloud space, and I have no access to it.

I don't run a server, and your recipes are never sent to me.

Opening recipe websites

When you open a saved recipe link, the website loads inside the app, the same way it would in Safari. That website can see the visit as it would any other, under its own privacy policy. Repertoire doesn't record which links you open.

Tips

You can leave an optional tip from the About screen. Apple handles the payment through the App Store. I don't receive your name, email address or card details, only the anonymous sales totals Apple gives every developer. Tips don't unlock anything.

Reviews

The app may occasionally show Apple's standard prompt asking you to rate it. Apple handles the prompt and any rating you leave.

What Repertoire doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No selling or sharing of your data

Emailing me

If you email me, I'll see your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply. You can ask me to delete our emails at any time.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the recipes stored on that device. To remove the copy in iCloud, go to Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage), choose Repertoire and delete its data.

Children

Repertoire doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact
