1. What TrailMatch does

TrailMatch matches the activities you have recorded on Strava against the routes of UK long-distance trails, and shows how much of each trail you have covered. You can also mark stretches as done by hand.

Matching is automatic and based on GPS recordings and published trail routes, both of which can be inaccurate. Progress figures are an estimate for your own interest. TrailMatch is not a navigation aid and should not be relied on for route finding or safety. Trail routes change, and you are responsible for your own safety outdoors.
