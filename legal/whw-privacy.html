West Highland Way Explorer - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

West Highland Way Explorer is a scratch-off map and guide for the West Highland Way in Scotland. You mark the sections you have walked, tick off places in the guide and keep a trail diary. This policy explains what data the app collects, who processes it, and how you can control it.

You can use the map and guide without an account. If you sign in, your walked sections, visited places and diary are saved to your account so they are kept safe and appear on any device you sign in on.

Who is responsible

West Highland Way Explorer is made by Natasha Wilson, an independent app developer trading as a sole trader in the United Kingdom. For the purposes of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, Natasha Wilson is the data controller.

Contact: flourishtechcoaching@gmail.com

Your account

Creating an account is optional. If you sign up, the app uses Google Firebase Authentication to store:

  • Your email address
  • Your password, which Firebase stores in hashed form. We can never see it.
  • A unique user ID, and when the account was created and last signed in

We also keep a small profile with your email address and the display name you choose, if you set one in Edit profile.

What is saved to your account

When you are signed in, the following is stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your user ID:

  • The sections of the trail you mark as walked: where each one starts and ends, the date you walked it, and any name you give it
  • If you draw a detour off the main route, the line you traced on the map
  • The towns, villages and points of interest you mark as visited, with the date
  • Your diary entries: title, text, date, and the optional location you type in

Security rules on the database mean only your signed-in account can read or change this data. We do not read your diary or your progress, except where you ask for help with a problem and it is needed to fix it.

Data kept on your device

Some things stay on your iPhone or iPad and are never sent to us: the name you enter during onboarding, your units and map layer preferences, and whether you have finished onboarding. They are removed when you delete the app.

Location

Showing your position on the map is optional and off until you turn it on. When it is on, the app uses your location only to draw the blue dot on the Scratch Map. Your location is never recorded, never saved to your account and never sent to us. You can turn it off in the app's Settings, or in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security, then Location Services.

When you add a section by typing an address, or save a section on the map, the app uses Apple's map services to look up place names for the start and end points. Those lookups are about points on the trail, not about where you are.

Usage analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand which features people use, for example which tabs are opened and when a section is saved. Firebase links these events to a random app instance ID. It also receives basic device information such as model, iOS version, language and approximate country. Analytics events never include your email, your name, your diary text or your location.

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and error reports, so problems can be found and fixed. These include the device model, iOS version, app version and details of the error. They do not include your email, your name or your diary text.

The app does not show ads, does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

App integrity checks

The app uses Firebase App Check, which relies on Apple's App Attest, to confirm that requests to the database come from the real app. This uses a token from Apple about the app install, not about you.

Purchases

The Pro upgrade is an optional one-time purchase. Purchases are handled by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, which processes:

  • An app user ID. This is a random ID, or your account's user ID if you are signed in, so the purchase can follow you to other devices.
  • Purchase history
  • Country of purchase and basic device information

We never see or receive your payment card details. Those go to Apple alone.

Lawful basis

Storing your account, progress and diary is needed to provide the service you signed up for (contract). Analytics, crash reports and integrity checks are used to keep the app working and improve it (legitimate interests).

Children

West Highland Way Explorer is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will act on it.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EEA, you have the right to access, correct, or erase your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to data portability.

You can delete individual sections, visited places and diary entries in the app at any time. To delete everything, open the Account tab and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account, profile, walked sections, visited places and diary entries.

For anything else, contact us at flourishtechcoaching@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority. In the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office.

Data retention

Your account data is kept until you delete it, or delete your account. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google, and purchase records by RevenueCat and Apple, according to their own published policies, linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
